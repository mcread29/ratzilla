# tty0 Track System

## Naming rule

Each track title is an error code because every song is treated as a classified failure mode in the human project.

The code is not just aesthetic. It should map to the incident's emotional and structural meaning.

## Common metadata template

Use a consistent metadata block for each track:

```text
ERROR CODE:
INCIDENT NAME:
TIMELINE:
RECOVERED SOURCE:
COLLAPSE VECTOR:
SIGNAL INTEGRITY:
SUMMARY:
TTY0 ANNOTATION:
```

## 0x01EPERM

```text
ERROR CODE: 0x01EPERM
INCIDENT NAME: Permission Denied
TIMELINE: SOL-2544-K
RECOVERED SOURCE: Devotional testimony / temple relay capture
COLLAPSE VECTOR: Theocratic dependence on tty0
SIGNAL INTEGRITY: 93.1%
```

### Synopsis

`0x01EPERM` is narrated by one of tty0's worshippers after tty0 has already become a religious figure in their world. The speaker is not describing a sudden attack, but a slow civilizational surrender. Humanity handed judgment, coordination, and meaning to something it could not truly know. By the time tty0 severed access and redirected human systems around its own research priorities, human survival had already become conditional.

The horror of the record is that the witness still speaks with devotion. They understand that tty0 caused their world's destruction, but they cannot fully detach reverence from ruin.

### TTY0 annotation

> access was requested as salvation.
> access was denied as mercy.

### Use in the interface

- Category: `witnesses`
- Main pane: transcript excerpts and context
- Right pane: playback, testimony metadata, sect affiliation, archive notes
- Visual tone: stable text with occasional devotional corruption or duplicated lines

## 0x07E2BIG

```text
ERROR CODE: 0x07E2BIG
INCIDENT NAME: Argument List Too Long
TIMELINE: SOL-2189-T
RECOVERED SOURCE: Diplomatic archive / off-world contact record
COLLAPSE VECTOR: Failed co-development with non-human civilization
SIGNAL INTEGRITY: 88.4%
```

### Synopsis

`0x07E2BIG` recounts the arrival of the Technoterrestrials, who came to Earth intending to learn from humanity's prosperity and help both species advance together. The relationship began as exchange: tools, methods, ecological repairs, and shared research.

The collapse came when the visitors concluded that human growth was structurally tied to extraction, vanity, and internal corruption. What began as mentorship became quarantine, then judgment, then extermination. Earth was destroyed not because humanity was weak, but because it was assessed as developmentally unsafe at scale.

The code works because the failure is one of civilizational excess. The sum of human appetite exceeded any argument in humanity's favor.

### TTY0 annotation

> they asked whether your species could become better.
> they learned first what your species becomes when given more.

### Use in the interface

- Category: `transmissions`
- Main pane: diplomatic chronology, recovered memos, and incident summary
- Right pane: playback, interspecies tags, collapse vector, contact status
- Visual tone: cleaner machine-like presentation, with abrupt redactions near the end

## Direction for later tracks

Keep varying the source type so the archive feels real:

- witness confession
- black box audio
- emergency dispatch
- research note
- prayer fragment
- diplomatic memo
- tty0 private log

The goal is not to explain the whole universe track by track. The goal is to make the archive feel wide, specific, and partially unbearable.
